04 — ABOUT

Just me,
my kitchen,
and your cake.

Hi, I'm the person behind CAKEPANO. I started baking for friends and family and never stopped. What began as a weekend hobby slowly became the thing I care about most.

I work alone. There's no team, no production line, no shortcuts. Every cake that leaves my kitchen has been touched, tasted, and finished by one pair of hands — mine. That means I take a limited number of orders each month so I can give every one my full attention.

I use only natural ingredients: seasonal fruit, real butter, no artificial flavours. The sweetness is always balanced, never overwhelming.

05 — HOW TO ORDER

Simple process,
personal service.

01

Reach Out

Send me a message on Instagram or via the contact form. Tell me the occasion, date, and any ideas you have in mind.

02

We Talk

I'll reply within 24 hours. We discuss flavours, size, design, and dietary needs. No detail is too small.

03

Confirm & Deposit

Once we agree on the details, I send a quote. A 50% deposit secures your date on my calendar.

04

I Bake

Your cake is made fresh, from scratch, by me alone — the morning before your collection or delivery

05

You Enjoy

Pick up or receive your cake. Every box leaves my kitchen with care instructions and a little note.
